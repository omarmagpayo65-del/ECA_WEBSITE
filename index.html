About ECA Advocacy

What is Engaged Citizenship Advocacy (ECA)?

Engaged Citizenship Advocacy (ECA) is a dynamic movement dedicated to cultivating active, responsible, and informed citizens. We empower individuals—especially youth leaders—to participate meaningfully in civic governance, promote community accountability, and drive sustainable grassroots development.

Our Mission

To foster active civic participation through education, community programs, leadership training, and transparent governance initiatives, building resilient communities where every citizen's voice matters.

Our Vision

A progressive society driven by empowered, socially conscious citizens committed to ethical leadership, sustainable change, and collaborative nation-building.

Privacy Policy & System Permissions

Data Privacy & User Protection

At Engaged Citizenship Advocacy (ECA), we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data in compliance with data privacy standards.

  • Data Collection: We collect only necessary details during registration strictly for membership verification and community updates.
  • Account Security: Passwords are securely handled via Firebase database architecture, and member credentials are restricted to authorized administrators.
  • Media Permissions: Photos and videos uploaded to community feeds or albums are moderated and displayed solely for advocacy documentation.
  • Display Preferences: You can customize your visual display themes via the settings panel at any time.
